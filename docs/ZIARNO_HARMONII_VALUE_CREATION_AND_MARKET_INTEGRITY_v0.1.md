> Purpose: build the conditions for durable, utility-led demand and credible market value. This document does not set, promise or guarantee a token price.

## 1. The objective

The objective is not to manufacture a high quote through scarcity theatre, hype or market manipulation. It is to make ZH useful enough that independent users and partners may voluntarily want to hold or use it, while keeping the initiative's public-good mission and human rights outside token ownership.

A high unit price alone is not proof of value. The relevant measures are:
- real and recurring use of the token;
- independently verified demand from users and partners;
- transparent circulating supply and unlock schedule;
- adequate, honestly disclosed liquidity;
- security, legal compliance and credible governance;
- measurable delivery by the ecosystem.

## 2. Price mathematics — no false promises

Market price is discovered in the market. A simplified relationship is:

**Market capitalization = circulating supply × market price**

**Fully diluted valuation (FDV) = maximum supply × market price**

With the current proposed maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 ZH, the following are mathematical illustrations only, not price targets or forecasts:

| Hypothetical FDV | Implied price per ZH |
|---:|---:|
| $1,000,000 | $0.001 |
| $10,000,000 | $0.01 |
| $100,000,000 | $0.10 |
| $1,000,000,000 | $1.00 |
| $10,000,000,000 | $10.00 |

These figures do not mean that the project is worth those amounts, that buyers exist, or that the token can be sold at those prices. Thin liquidity can make quoted prices misleading.

Changing the number of token units (including a redenomination or reverse split) changes the unit price mathematically but does not create economic value. Burning tokens does not guarantee appreciation and must never be marketed as a price-support mechanism.

## 3. Demand must come from delivered utility

Before any public issuance, the project should demonstrate at least two independently usable, functioning utilities. Candidate utilities, subject to product, partner and legal approval:

1. **Optional partner services:** a signed partner agreement specifying exactly what service can be accessed or discounted using ZH.
2. **Optional ecosystem features:** clearly defined digital services where ZH has a genuine function beyond speculation.
3. **Verified contribution recognition:** transparent rules for recognizing useful contributions. Rewards must not buy research conclusions, ecological verification, governance control or personal data.
4. **Interoperable integrations:** documented APIs and audited smart-contract integrations with real participating applications.

Non-negotiable boundary: core public information, safety resources, the right to participate in discussion, the right to leave, and basic human participation in NEURAL LINK must remain available without buying or holding ZH.

No utility may be described as live until it is implemented and independently testable. No partner may be named as accepting ZH without a signed agreement.

## 4. Supply discipline and distribution

The existing tokenomics v0.3 proposes a maximum supply of 1B ZH and seven allocation buckets. This plan does not change those allocations.

Before any launch:
- publish the exact circulating supply at launch and every wallet/category receiving tokens, subject to lawful privacy limits;
- publish vesting contracts, unlock dates and release conditions;
- lock treasury and vesting allocations in independently reviewed contracts;
- use independent multisignature custody and public transaction reporting;
- disclose insider, partner and treasury holdings and conflicts of interest;
- prohibit discretionary minting beyond the approved cap;
- do not create artificial scarcity by hiding supply or misleading users about unlocks.

A lower initial circulating supply is not, by itself, a value strategy. It must be justified by genuine staged delivery, fair distribution and transparent vesting—not by an attempt to create a misleading float.

## 5. Liquidity and market access

No exchange listing or liquidity pool should be pursued until:
- legal classification and the responsible issuer/offeror are established;
- the applicable EU/German MiCA and other legal requirements have been assessed in writing;
- the public documentation and disclosures are complete;
- the smart contract and custody architecture have passed independent review;
- a separate liquidity policy states the venue, pair, funding source, custody, risks, conflicts and reporting;
- users are told clearly that liquidity may be limited and that they may be unable to sell.

The proposed 15% liquidity reserve in tokenomics v0.3 is only an unissued planning allocation. It is not cash, collateral, a price floor or a promise of a liquidity pool.

## 6. Market-integrity prohibitions

ZH must not use:
- wash trading, fake volume, spoofing, coordinated pump groups or self-trading;
- undisclosed paid promotion, fake endorsements or fabricated partnerships;
- false claims of guaranteed returns, guaranteed listing, guaranteed liquidity or a target price;
- hidden insider allocations, undisclosed unlocks or misleading supply figures;
- buybacks, burns or treasury transactions advertised as guaranteed price support;
- pressure tactics or claims that people must buy ZH to support the mission.

All public statements must distinguish current facts, proposed features, forecasts and risks.

## 7. Evidence dashboard required before any launch decision

Publish a dated dashboard with:
- total maximum supply, circulating supply and locked supply;
- wallet concentration and scheduled unlocks;
- number of functioning utilities (not roadmap promises);
- number of signed partner integrations and what each actually delivers;
- monthly active users and repeat usage, with privacy-preserving measurement;
- actual token utility transactions, excluding wash/self-transactions;
- liquidity depth, spread and trading volume, with venue and measurement period;
- treasury balances, movements, grants and independent reviews;
- security audit status and unresolved findings;
- legal review and publication status.

No single metric should be presented as proof of value. Report negative and zero results as well as positive results.

## 10. Regulatory baseline

For an in-scope public offer or admission to trading of a crypto-asset other than an asset-referenced token or e-money token, MiCA Title II sets requirements including a legal-person offeror, a crypto-asset white paper, notification/publication and compliant marketing communications, subject to the Regulation's scope and exemptions. Article 7 requires relevant marketing communications to be identifiable, fair, clear, not misleading and consistent with the white paper where required. The exact classification and obligations for ZH must be assessed by qualified EU/German counsel before any offer, distribution or admission-to-trading activity.
