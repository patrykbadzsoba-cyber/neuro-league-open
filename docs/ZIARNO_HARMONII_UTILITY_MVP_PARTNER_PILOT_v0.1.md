# ZIARNO HARMONII — UTILITY MVP & PARTNER PILOT SPECIFICATION v0.1
**Initiative:** NEURAL LINK / NEURO LEAGUE OPEN  
**Token:** Ziarno Harmonii (ZH)  
**Date:** 18 November 2026  
**Status:** DESIGN SPECIFICATION — NO TOKEN ISSUANCE OR PARTNER COMMITMENT

## 1. Purpose

Define the smallest credible, testable utility pilot that could create genuine reasons to use ZH. The pilot must demonstrate a real service, a real partner, a real user journey and verifiable usage before any public token offer or market launch is considered.

This is not a price-support scheme. It does not promise a market price, profit, liquidity, redemption value or appreciation.

## 2. Proposed first utility: optional partner-service access

A participating independent partner may choose to accept ZH for a clearly specified optional service, benefit or discount. The partner must define the service, eligibility, quantity/availability, ZH amount (if any), expiry, refund/cancellation treatment and applicable consumer terms in a signed agreement.

Examples of service categories for partner discussions (not existing offers):
- optional digital features or event add-ons;
- optional educational or community workshops;
- optional partner-provided services that do not determine scientific outcomes or access to essential public resources.

No partner is currently represented as accepting ZH. No goods or services are promised by this specification.

## 3. Pilot architecture

### Roles
- **User:** connects a compatible wallet and chooses whether to use ZH for the stated optional service.
- **Partner:** independently decides whether to accept ZH and fulfils the service.
- **Application:** displays the partner's current terms, quote and transaction status.
- **Treasury/custody operator:** if introduced, operates under a published legal and multisignature policy; no unilateral personal control.
- **Independent reviewer:** checks that pilot reporting is accurate and that token use does not buy influence over research or ecological verification.

### Transaction flow
1. Partner publishes a signed service listing with exact terms and ZH amount, if applicable.
2. User reviews terms and confirms the optional service.
3. Application generates a transaction request; it never signs or transfers without the user's explicit wallet confirmation.
4. Partner verifies finality and fulfils the service.
5. Application records a privacy-minimised receipt containing transaction hash, listing ID, timestamp, status and dispute/refund reference.
6. Monthly pilot report publishes aggregate usage, failed transactions, fulfilment and complaints, without exposing personal wallet-to-identity links.

### Required application states
- Listing active / paused / expired.
- Quote available / unavailable.
- Awaiting user signature.
- Submitted / confirmed / failed.
- Fulfilment pending / fulfilled / disputed / refunded (where technically and legally possible).

## 4. What must be built

### Minimum product scope
- Partner directory with verified status and clear terms.
- Individual service listing with a versioned terms record.
- Wallet connection using a standard wallet provider; no custody of user keys.
- User-confirmed transaction initiation.
- Chain and token contract verification before display.
- Transaction status tracking.
- Partner fulfilment confirmation.
- Support/dispute contact route.
- Privacy notice and data-minimisation controls.
- Public aggregate pilot dashboard.

### Excluded from MVP
- Exchange listing, DEX pool, price chart or price target.
- Yield, staking rewards, lending, borrowing or revenue sharing.
- Buybacks, price floors, guaranteed redemption or token burns marketed as value support.
- Token-weighted control over NEURAL LINK, people, personal data, scientific findings or ecological outcomes.
- Sale of ecological credits, land rights, water rights or restoration results.
- Automatic transfer or spending without user confirmation.
- Claims that the utility is live before it is deployed and tested.

## 5. Partner acceptance criteria

A partner is eligible for the pilot only if all conditions are met:
- legal identity and authorised signatory verified;
- specific existing service is identified and available to users;
- written agreement describes the service and acceptance conditions;
- no claim of guaranteed token value or investment return;
- refund, complaint, consumer and tax responsibilities allocated;
- data processing and privacy roles documented;
- service can be fulfilled without compromising human dignity or the public-good mission;
- no influence over research conclusions, environmental measurements or independent verification is granted;
- partner may pause acceptance with clear notice and treatment of pending transactions.

A verbal expression of interest is not acceptance. A logo, conversation or draft email is not a signed partnership.

## 6. Pilot success metrics

The pilot is evidence-producing only if it reports:
- number of signed partners and active service listings;
- number of eligible users and completed user journeys;
- completed utility transactions (excluding self-transactions and wash activity);
- fulfilment rate and median fulfilment time;
- failed, disputed and refunded transactions;
- repeat use by cohort, using privacy-preserving metrics;
- partner retention and user satisfaction;
- on-chain fees and total user costs;
- concentration of usage and any conflicts of interest.

Do not optimise for token price or trading volume during the utility pilot. Publish zero or negative results honestly.

## 7. Legal and operational gates

Before any real-value use:
1. Obtain written EU/German legal advice on token classification and the proposed service/payment flow.
2. Determine who is the legal issuer, offeror, partner and responsible operator.
3. Confirm whether the planned offer falls within MiCA and whether any exemption actually applies. Do not assume that calling ZH a utility token creates an exemption.
4. In particular, MiCA Article 4(3)(c) refers to a utility token providing access to a good or service that exists or is in operation. Article 4(6) limits the described offer period to 12 months where the utility service does not yet exist or is not yet in operation. A qualified legal assessment is required for the exact facts.
5. If an offer or admission to trading is in scope, complete the applicable white paper, notification, publication, marketing and offeror requirements before proceeding.
6. Complete smart-contract threat modelling, testing and independent audit.
7. Publish partner terms, user risks, fees, privacy information and support route.
8. Obtain separate governance authorisation for mainnet deployment, token issuance and any public offer.

Official MiCA sources:
- Article 4 — Offers to the public: https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-4-offers-public-crypto-assets-other
- Regulation (EU) 2023/1114: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng

## 8. First partner pilot request — required proposal contents

The first approach to a prospective partner should ask only for:
- confirmation whether the partner is open to exploring an optional ZH utility pilot;
- one existing service that could be made optionally available through the pilot;
- operational requirements, costs, availability and fulfilment process;
- legal/compliance contact for reviewing a non-binding pilot outline;
- permission to prepare a draft memorandum of understanding.

The approach must state that no token has been issued, there is no live contract or market, and the request is exploratory and non-binding. Do not claim that the partner is already part of NEURAL LINK.

- [ ] At least one partner has signed a pilot agreement.
- [ ] At least one existing service is available and documented.
- [ ] Application flow is implemented and tested on a test network.
- [ ] Wallet signing is user-controlled and no private keys are collected.
