# NEURAL LINK — PLANET RESTORATION FUND — EXCLUSIVE PROJECT CARD v0.1
**Status:** MODEL APPROVED FOR PREPARATION — NOT FUNDED, NOT ISSUED  
**Cardholder:** One named, individually authorized project operator (the founder/operator designated by the Fund)  
**Funding owner / account holder:** Planet Restoration Fund or its legally accountable sponsoring entity  
**Purpose:** Pay approved costs required to initiate and implement NEURAL LINK and planet-restoration work  
**Date:** 18 November 2026

## 1. Decision captured

The card is **for one person only**: the designated NEURAL LINK project operator. It is not a public card, community card, partner card, or general benefit for token holders.

The card is linked to a dedicated account or formally ring-fenced funding facility belonging to the Planet Restoration Fund or its legally accountable fiscal sponsor. The named operator receives one card and authority to use it only for approved project expenditure.

The NEURAL LINK mission, principles and identity do not change. The card is an operational instrument to make real-world implementation possible.

## 2. Core structure

- **Fund:** Planet Restoration Fund (working name; legal entity / fiscal sponsor must be established or confirmed).
- **Account:** A dedicated project account or segregated account ledger under the Fund/sponsor's legal ownership and control.
- **Card:** One business debit or charge card issued to the named operator, with the Fund/sponsor as account holder or responsible contracting entity as permitted by the issuer.
- **Sole cardholder:** One named operator. No additional cards or cardholders under this model.
- **Funding:** Donations, grants, sponsorships, partner contributions, or another lawful source actually received and accepted by the Fund/sponsor. ZH is not treated as cash, collateral, or guaranteed purchasing power unless a regulated provider separately accepts it under written terms.
- **Payment:** The card spends only available funded balance or a separately approved credit/charge facility. No assumption of unlimited credit or automatic future repayment.
- **Reporting:** Every transaction is recorded against a project, budget line, date, supplier, amount and supporting invoice/receipt.

## 3. Intended eligible expenditure

Subject to the approved budget, funding agreement and applicable law:
- travel and transport necessary for project implementation and partner meetings;
- accommodation required for approved project travel or field work;
- reasonable meals/per-diem costs where permitted by the funding agreement and tax rules;
- equipment, tools, communications and software needed for project delivery;
- fieldwork, sampling, measurement, safety and restoration materials;
- venue, logistics and other documented implementation costs.

The card is not for personal shopping, private rent, unrelated debts, cash withdrawals, speculative investments, token purchases, or private living costs unless a specific cost is expressly approved as an eligible project cost under the governing funding agreement and law.

## 4. Invoicing and expense evidence

Preferred workflow:
1. Before a material purchase, confirm that the cost is in the approved project budget and that sufficient funds are available.
2. Ask the supplier to issue the invoice to the Fund/sponsor's legal name and address, with the relevant project reference, wherever the supplier and local rules permit.
3. Pay with the single project card.
4. Save the invoice, receipt, booking confirmation and proof of payment.
5. Record the business purpose, project/workstream, date, supplier and amount.
6. Reconcile the card statement monthly and make records available to the Fund/sponsor's authorized reviewer.

A card payment alone does not make an expense eligible. Grant or sponsor terms may impose stricter rules, prior approvals, per-diem limits, procurement rules, or a two-person review. Where required, those rules prevail.

## 5. No personal liability by default

The intended arrangement is that the Fund/sponsor funds and controls the account and is responsible for the card contract. The operator is an authorized user, not the borrower or guarantor.

This must be confirmed in the card agreement and written authorization. The operator must not be asked to sign a personal guarantee, accept personal credit liability, or cover project expenditure from private funds as a condition of using the card.

If an issuer only offers a personal credit card or requires the operator to be personally liable, that product does not meet this model and must not be presented as the solution.

## 6. Funding model

The card cannot be activated until actual funds or a legally binding facility are in place. Potential funding sources:
- grants and institutional project funding;
- unrestricted or purpose-restricted donations accepted by the Fund/sponsor;
- corporate sponsorship and partner contributions;
- in-kind support for transport, accommodation, equipment or services;
- a separately negotiated credit/charge facility contracted by the Fund/sponsor, not assumed to exist.

The Fund must not spend unreceived pledges. No supplier, card issuer or operator should be told that ZH has a guaranteed cash value unless this is legally and contractually true.

## 7. ZH relationship

ZH remains a proposed ecosystem-utility token with a 1,000,000,000 maximum-supply preparation model. It is not itself the bank account, card balance, legal tender, credit line, guarantee, or proof that the Fund has assets.

The Planet Restoration Fund may publish transparent reports on approved ZH-related ecosystem activity only after the legal and accounting model is reviewed. No ZH issuance, sale, collateralization, conversion or promise of redemption is authorized by this card specification.

## 8. Governance and safeguards

- The cardholder is one person only.
- The Fund/sponsor remains the account owner and budget authority.
- A written delegation defines eligible categories, transaction limits, approval thresholds, emergency rules and suspension rights.
- No single person has unilateral control of the Fund's bank account and treasury.
- Independent review and periodic reconciliation are required.
- The operator receives a clear, workable spending authority within the approved budget; controls must not make field implementation impracticable.
- Lost card, suspected fraud or disputed transactions are reported immediately to the issuer and Fund/sponsor.
- No private keys, seed phrases or token signing credentials are connected to the card.

## 9. Legal and operational prerequisites

Before application or activation:
- identify the legal owner/operator of the Planet Restoration Fund or secure a fiscal sponsor;
- establish the legal name, address, registration/tax status and authorized representatives;
- open or designate the dedicated project account;
- adopt the project budget and eligible-cost policy;
- obtain written approval naming the single cardholder and delegated authority;
- choose an issuer that supports a card for one authorized employee/representative while the organization remains liable;
- complete issuer KYC/KYB and source-of-funds checks;
- review German/EU accounting, tax, employment/volunteer, grant and payment-services requirements;
- confirm whether invoices must be addressed to the Fund/sponsor and what evidence is required by each funder.

A project without its own legal personality may need an established nonprofit or other suitable organization to act as fiscal sponsor. The sponsor must expressly agree to hold funds, contract with the issuer, authorize the cardholder and administer reporting.

## 10. Launch gates

- [ ] Fund legal entity or fiscal sponsor identified and consenting.
- [ ] Funding received or binding facility approved.
- [ ] Dedicated account established in Fund/sponsor's name.
- [ ] Approved initial budget and eligible expenditure policy adopted.
- [ ] One named cardholder formally authorized.
- [ ] Issuer confirms account-holder liability remains with Fund/sponsor and no personal guarantee is required.
- [ ] KYC/KYB and compliance checks passed.
- [ ] Card agreement, limits, dispute process and monthly reconciliation procedure approved.

Until all gates are met, the card is a prepared operating model only. No card has been issued, no account has been opened, no funds are confirmed, and no spending limit is available.
