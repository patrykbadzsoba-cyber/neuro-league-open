Autonomous hotel parking

STAYFLOW SMART VALET
🚘

Arrive. Leave. Your car parks itself.

No valet handover and no tip. At a participating hotel, the parking infrastructure detects your vehicle, checks the route and coordinates its movement to a free space. Request it back when you are ready to leave.

Concept preview only. No live cameras, sensors, vehicle controls or parking operator are connected in this demo.

Seamless arrival & luggage delivery

STAYFLOW ROOM CONCIERGE
🧳

Leave the luggage to us

A separate baggage service coordinates collection at the arrival bay, secure identification, transport and delivery to your room. Your car and luggage follow two independent, coordinated workflows.

Arrival sequence

1 · Vehicle handover
The parking system receives the vehicle after the guest and passengers have exited.
2 · Separate luggage collection
A dedicated baggage unit identifies the booking, confirms the boot is accessible and records each bag. Robotic unloading requires a compatible vehicle, authorized access and a validated manipulation system.
3 · Secure room delivery
A luggage robot or automated cart transports bags through approved hotel routes. Access to lifts and guest-only areas is coordinated with hotel systems.
4 · Room ready
StayFlow coordinates room readiness, climate and lighting preferences, and optional welcome service.
Demo only — no baggage robot, trunk access, lift or room system is connected.
