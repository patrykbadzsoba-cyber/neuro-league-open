# NEURO LEAGUE OPEN — Institutional Briefing
**13 November 2026 · Public working document · Version 1.0**

## Purpose
This briefing introduces an open, non-commercial initiative focused on human dignity, cooperation, responsible technology, and protection and regeneration of living systems. It distinguishes documented concepts from work not yet implemented or independently verified.

**Core principle: LIFE IS THE CENTER.** Technology should serve life; people remain in control.

## 1. Overview and origins
NEURO LEAGUE OPEN grew from the question: can people cooperate to protect our shared home rather than continue processes that damage it? The initiative was proposed as an open global framework grounded in freedom of thought, human dignity, autonomy, cooperation, and technology serving life.

NEURAL LINK is the human-first cooperation framework. AI may assist with research, translation, evidence organization, and planning, but it does not own the initiative, represent people without authorization, or replace scientific review and accountable human decisions.

The public idea-origin register distinguishes the initiator’s concepts from AI-assisted drafting and technical elaboration. Discussion or documentation is not proof of scientific, legal, or operational approval.

## 2. Main areas of work

### NEUROHARMONY — Understanding the Chemistry of Life
A proposed research and educational area covering neurochemistry, emotions and perception, neuroplasticity, sleep and stress, and social and environmental influences on the brain. **Status: conceptual research direction.** No approved clinical study, diagnosis, treatment, or medical benefit is claimed.

### GLOBAL EXPERIMENT — environmental diagnosis and restoration
A proposed staged process: baseline diagnosis → analysis → restoration options → risk assessment → actions authorized by responsible parties → monitoring → verification. The atmospheric-restoration methodology identifies relevant pollutants and source categories, calls for reliable measurements and uncertainty reporting, and prioritizes reducing emissions at source.

### TAR-001 — Tarpenbek catchment pilot concept
A place-based ecological pilot concept for the Tarpenbek catchment in the Norderstedt/Hamburg area. Proposed sequence: baseline diagnosis and documentation → restoration plan → transparent resource accounting → traceable action records → verification. **Status: planning and verification preparation; no completed or independently verified restoration is claimed.**

### GLOBAL DNB / EKO KONCERT
A cultural concept connecting music, international cooperation, and environmental responsibility. The proposed event and energy specifications remain concepts requiring technical design, permissions, and measurement; they are not presented as delivered outcomes.

## 3. Participation, consent, and protection
No participant recruitment or human-subject data collection is currently claimed through the repository. Any future activity involving people must have a separate, readable participant information sheet and consent process before recruitment or data collection. It must specify the exact activity, responsible contact, data collected and purpose, storage/access/deletion, foreseeable risks, voluntary participation, withdrawal rights, compensation (if any), and any required ethics review or authorization.

The initiative does not claim completed ethics approval or institutional authorization. Participation must be voluntary, refusal must carry no penalty, and people must be able to leave. No sensitive personal information should be submitted through unapproved channels.

## 4. Transparency: what exists and what does not
**Documented:** project principles, origin register, AI participation protocol, atmospheric-restoration methodology, and public status register.

**Proposed / in preparation:** NEUROHARMONY research direction, TAR-001 field pilot, global environmental-restoration workflow, and future participant protocol.

**Not claimed as implemented:** a clinically approved human study; verified environmental restoration; active participant enrolment; a shared user database; user accounts; verified identities; private messaging; server-side moderation; or a live multi-user Commons service.

The current portal is a static frontend. Its Commons composer stores drafts in the visitor’s own browser only; it does not publish posts to a shared server or transmit messages to other visitors. The public status register notes that standalone website deployment has not yet been verified.

## 5. Funding, tokens, and ownership
The TAR-001 financial gate is inactive. The current plan does not authorize token issuance, promises of profit or investment returns, sale of ownership or rights to results, or active fundraising before relevant work and results are verified. Reading and reviewing the public documentation requires no payment.

The initiative is intended to remain open and non-commercial. Voluntary support considered in the future must not confer ownership, control, privileged access, or rights over people, data, or ecological outcomes. Specific legal and governance arrangements remain to be developed and reviewed.

## 6. AI participation and governance
AI may explain technical material, translate, organize evidence, identify uncertainty, help design research questions, and compare restoration options. AI must not invent participants, partners, endorsements, permissions, field results, or scientific consensus. Decisions, consent, and accountability remain with people and competent authorities.
