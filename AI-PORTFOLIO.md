## Selected projects

### 1. PV Calculator — Germany
Repository: https://github.com/patrykbadzsoba-cyber/Kalkulator-fotowoltaiczny-

A brand-neutral photovoltaic planning calculator for homeowners in Germany.

**Implemented/documented capabilities**
- Estimates PV system size from annual electricity use and usable roof area.
- Models roof orientation and tilt using simplified planning factors.
- Estimates annual yield, monthly distribution, self-consumption, grid export, optional battery storage, financial benefit, and simple payback.
- Supports editable electricity price and feed-in tariff assumptions.
- Provides printable result report and CSV export.
- Responsive interface and German-language presentation.

**Engineering judgement demonstrated**
- Documents assumptions and uncertainty instead of presenting estimates as guarantees.
- Separates browser-side calculations from external geocoding/weather requests.
- Explicitly documents privacy implications, commercial-use limitations, and the not-yet-verified PVGIS proxy deployment.
