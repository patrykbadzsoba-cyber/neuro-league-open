The collaboration history and project artefacts show examples of:

- Breaking broad goals into smaller deliverables and implementation steps.
- Iterating on requirements and correcting outputs that do not satisfy constraints.
- Asking AI systems to explain assumptions, limitations, and actual implementation status.
- Maintaining project principles across multiple conversations and revisions.
- Comparing and considering feedback from more than one AI system.
- Treating privacy, user autonomy, transparency, and the ability to leave a system as design requirements.
- Preferring verifiable actions and concrete outputs over unsupported claims.

These are qualitative observations from the available record, not results from a standardized assessment.
